# **VA Community Care (VA TMS)**

If your local VA facility cannot provide timely care or doesn't offer advanced mental health treatments on-site, you don't have to go without help. Through the VA Community Care program, eligible veterans can receive Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) from approved local providers, fully authorized and covered by the VA.

[First cleared by the FDA](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/recently-approved-devices/transcranial-magnetic-stimulation-tms), TMS is a proven, non-invasive option recognized for veterans struggling with major depressive disorder when traditional antidepressants haven't provided relief.

&nbsp;

## **What Is VA Community Care (VA TMS)?**

VA Community Care (VA TMS) is a program that allows veterans to receive transcranial magnetic stimulation therapy from approved non-VA community providers when local VA facilities cannot provide timely access to care. [The VA describes TMS](https://www.mentalhealth.va.gov/tms/index.asp) as a noninvasive treatment that uses magnetic pulses to stimulate brain areas involved in mood regulation.&nbsp;

Through this pathway, eligible veterans can access advanced mental health care closer to home, while the VA remains fully responsible for authorizing, coordinating, and paying for the treatment course.

### **How Do You Navigate the VA Community Care Process?**

Getting authorized for outside care can feel daunting, but the pathway is straightforward once you know the steps. The process relies entirely on establishing a clinical need and confirming that your local VA clinic cannot fulfill that need within standard wait-time or drive-time windows.

* **Step 1: The Consult** – Your primary VA doctor or psychiatrist submits an official consult request for TMS therapy.  
* **Step 2: Review & Eligibility** – The VA referral team evaluates if the treatment can be provided internally. If it cannot, they open the Community Care pathway.  
* **Step 3: Authorization** – Once approved, the VA issues a formal authorization. At this point, you can explicitly request an in-network partner like **Liberty TMS**.  
* **Step 4: Your Care Begins** – You attend your daily sessions at our clinic while we handle the data sharing and paperwork directly with the VA.

### **What Should You Expect During Your TMS Sessions?**

TMS is completely non-invasive, meaning there are no needles, no surgery, and zero downtime. Because it requires no anesthesia or sedation, you remain fully awake and alert throughout the entire process, allowing you to drive yourself home or go right back to work immediately after.

* **Daily Routine:** Treatment consists of quick 20 to 30-minute sessions, scheduled 5 days a week for a standard 4 to 6-week course.  
* **The Experience:** You will sit comfortably in a reclining chair while a magnetic coil is gently placed against your scalp. As the targeted magnetic pulses stimulate underactive areas of the brain responsible for mood regulation, you will feel a light tapping sensation.  
* **During Treatment:** You are free to read, listen to music, watch a show, or simply relax while the technology does the work.

**Do I Qualify for VA-Authorized TMS Therapy?**&nbsp;

Approval for community-based treatment relies on meeting both clinical safety standards and the VA's administrative access guidelines.

**Clinical Qualifications**&nbsp;

To be deemed a candidate for this treatment, the VA typically requires:

* A documented diagnosis of Major Depressive Disorder (MDD).  
* A history of an inadequate response to at least one standard antidepressant medication trial.  
* No active history of psychosis or unmanaged seizure disorders.

**Important Safety Contraindications** Because TMS uses targeted magnetic pulses, it cannot be safely administered if you have certain medical devices or implants. You should not undergo TMS if you have:

* Pacemakers, ICDs, or deep brain stimulators.  
* Ferromagnetic metal, bullet fragments, or metallic plates in the head or neck area (dental fillings and braces are perfectly safe).  
* Severe, unmanaged cardiovascular disease.

## **What Kind of Results Can Veterans Expect?**&nbsp;

For those who feel like they have exhausted every option, TMS offers a completely non-pharmacological, non-systemic approach. It works directly on the root neurological pathways of depression without the heavy side effects common to oral medications, like weight gain, brain fog, or fatigue.

Veterans undergoing treatment routinely report widespread quality-of-life improvements, including:

* A noticeable lift in persistent low moods and daily anxiety.  
* Restored interest in hobbies, work, and reconnecting with family.  
* Significantly improved sleep patterns and morning energy levels.

### **The Data on Treatment-Resistant Depression**

Published clinical data on PubMed shows that TMS achieves a [50% to 60% response rate](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40709628/) in individuals who have previously seen no relief from antidepressants, with 30% to 40% achieving full clinical remission. These positive changes are sustained for months, and frequently years, following the completion of the initial care cycle.

## **What Are the Safety Profiles and Side Effects of VA TMS?**

TMS safety is well-established through decades of research. The VA recognizes TMS as safe and effective.

### **Common Side Effects**

Most veterans experience mild, temporary side effects: scalp discomfort, headache, lightheadedness, and muscle twitching.

### **Serious Side Effects**

Serious effects are rare. No anesthesia TMS eliminates sedation risks.

## **Understanding Costs, Insurance, and TRICARE Coverage**&nbsp;

Financial stress shouldn't stand between you and mental health recovery. We ensure your coverage details are crystal clear before your first session begins.

* **VA Community Care Coverage:** When your referral is authorized in advance through the Community Care network, the VA completely covers the cost of your sessions, leaving you with minimal to zero out-of-pocket expenses.  
* **Out-of-Network Costs:** To put the value of this benefit in perspective, an out-of-pocket, self-funded course of TMS therapy typically ranges between $6,000 and $12,000 nationwide.  
* **TRICARE Insurance:** If you are covered via TRICARE rather than direct VA benefits, the program fully covers TMS for adults meeting clinical criteria. Additionally, following recent policy updates, TRICARE expanded its coverage parameters to include adolescents aged 15 and older for Major Depressive Disorder.

## **Frequently Asked Questions About VA Community Care (VA TMS)**

### **What is VA Community Care (VA TMS)?**

VA Community Care (VA TMS) is a program that allows eligible veterans to receive Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) therapy from approved, non-VA community providers when local VA facilities cannot deliver timely access to care. Under this pathway, veterans can access advanced mental health services closer to home, while the VA remains responsible for coordinating and covering the cost of treatment.

### **Am I eligible for VA TMS through Community Care?**

Eligibility relies on both clinical safety standards and the VA's administrative access guidelines. Clinically, the VA typically requires a documented diagnosis of Major Depressive Disorder (MDD) and a history of inadequate response to at least one standard antidepressant medication trial. Additionally, you must not have contraindications like implanted electronic devices or ferromagnetic metal in the head or neck area.

### **How do I get referred for VA TMS?**

The process begins with your primary VA doctor or psychiatrist submitting an official consult request for TMS therapy. The VA referral team will evaluate if the treatment can be provided internally. If it cannot due to wait times or distance, they will approve the [Community Care pathway](https://www.va.gov/communitycare/programs/veterans/index.asp), allowing you to request an in-network provider like Liberty TMS.

### **How long does VA TMS treatment last?**

A standard treatment course typically runs for 4 to 6 weeks, with quick 20 to 30-minute sessions conducted 5 days a week (averaging 20 to 30 sessions total). Because the procedure is completely non-invasive and requires no anesthesia, you will remain fully awake and can immediately return to your normal daily routine following each session.

### **Does VA Community Care cover TMS therapy?**

Yes. When your referral is authorized in advance through the Community Care network, the VA completely covers the cost of your sessions. This leaves approved veterans with minimal to zero out-of-pocket expenses for the entire duration of their authorized care cycle.

### **What are the side effects of VA TMS?**

Most veterans experience minimal side effects that are generally mild and transient. Common TMS side effects include mild scalp discomfort or tenderness at the stimulation site, temporary headache following treatment sessions, lightheadedness that resolves quickly, and scalp muscle twitching during pulse delivery. These effects are generally temporary and do not require stopping treatment. Serious side effects are rare. No anesthesia TMS eliminates sedation risks, and TMS without surgery avoids surgical complications.

### **Can I receive TMS for conditions other than depression through VA?**

The VA primarily approves TMS therapy for Major Depressive Disorder, though it may also be considered for Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder (OCD). While clinical research is ongoing for standalone conditions like anxiety or PTSD, coverage for them is not currently standard unless they co-occur alongside a primary MDD diagnosis.

### **What if my local VA doesn't offer TMS?**

If your local facility does not offer TMS on-site, this automatically opens the possibility for the Community Care pathway. You can request a consult from your VA provider to clear the way for treatment through an approved outside network clinic.

### **How do I choose a VA TMS provider?**

You can choose any authorized provider within the VA network. It is best to look for a clinic that has deep experience with TMS technology, offers flexible daily scheduling, and understands how to navigate the VA authorization process. At Liberty TMS, our team handles the heavy lifting of documentation and coordination directly with the VA on your behalf.
