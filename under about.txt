# **Accreditations & Certifications**

Liberty TMS delivers non-invasive Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation governed by physician oversight, rigorous clinical standards, and validated device benchmarks across North Carolina and Colorado. Maintaining authentic clinical accreditations & certifications ensures every patient, military family member, and referring provider receives safe, transparent, and evidence-based mental healthcare.

## **Clinical Oversight & Licensing Standards**

Every clinical assessment, motor threshold determination, and ongoing treatment course at Liberty TMS is supervised directly by licensed medical doctors specializing in psychiatry.

* **Psychiatrist-Directed Care:** Clinical protocols align with medical guidelines established by the American Psychiatric Association (APA) and the Clinical TMS Society.  
* **State Medical Licensing:** Attending physicians maintain active, unrestricted medical licenses governed by state regulatory authorities, including the North Carolina Medical Board and the Colorado Medical Board.  
* **Federal Provider Registry:** Liberty TMS clinics operate as credentialed healthcare facilities registered with the federal National Provider Identifier (NPI) registry.  
* **Facility Accreditations & Certifications:** Outpatient TMS practices operate under state healthcare facility standards, adhering to rigorous clinical safety benchmarks across all clinic locations.

## **FDA-Cleared NeuroStar® Technology**

Liberty TMS exclusively utilizes the NeuroStar Advanced Therapy System (manufactured by Neuronetics, Inc.) for outpatient clinical neuromodulation. As documented in the [FDA Medical Device Database](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpmn/pmn.cfm), NeuroStar holds clearances through the 510(k) regulatory pathway for three specific indications:

* **Major Depressive Disorder (MDD):** FDA-cleared in October 2008 for adult patients with treatment-resistant depression who failed to achieve satisfactory improvement from prior antidepressant medication.  
* **Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder (OCD):** FDA-cleared in May 2022 as an adjunctive neuromodulation treatment for adults.  
* **MDD with Comorbid Anxiety Symptoms (Anxious Depression):** FDA-cleared in July 2022 to treat anxiety symptoms associated with depression.

## **Certified Clinical Operators**

Daily TMS sessions are administered by healthcare professionals who have completed comprehensive, manufacturer-certified training directly through Neuronetics. Certified operators manage Contact Sensing technology, confirm coil alignment, and monitor patient comfort throughout treatment under continuous physician supervision.

## **Insurance Credentialing & Military Network Coverage**

Liberty TMS maintains credentialed provider relationships with major commercial, public, and military healthcare networks to ensure straightforward access to care:

* **TRICARE In-Network Status:** Formally credentialed with TRICARE East (Humana Military) and TRICARE West via [TRICARE](https://www.tricare.mil/), serving active-duty service members, retirees, and dependents near Fort Liberty, Fort Carson, Peterson SFB, Schriever SFB, and the U.S. Air Force Academy.

## **Frequently Asked Questions**

### **What accreditations & certifications verify patient safety at Liberty TMS?**

Patient care is governed by licensed psychiatric oversight, active state medical licensing in North Carolina and Colorado, registered federal provider credentials (NPI), and manufacturer-certified NeuroStar system operators.

### **Is NeuroStar TMS officially cleared by the FDA?**

Yes. NeuroStar Advanced Therapy holds clearance from the FDA for treatment-resistant Major Depressive Disorder (October 2008), Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder (May 2022), and major depressive disorder with comorbid anxiety (July 2022).

### **Who administers daily TMS therapy sessions?**

Sessions are administered by healthcare specialists who hold operator certification directly from the device manufacturer (Neuronetics). Treatment mapping and clinical management remain directed by licensed psychiatrists.

### **How does device clearance differ from clinic accreditation?**

FDA clearance validates that the medical equipment (NeuroStar) is proven safe and effective for indicated conditions. Clinical accreditations and credentials reflect the professional licensing, medical training, and operational standards of the clinicians delivering care.

### **Are Liberty TMS treatments covered by health insurance?**

Yes. Liberty TMS is an in-network provider for TRICARE East and TRICARE West, coordinates directly with the VA Community Care Network, and accepts major plans including Medicare, Blue Cross Blue Shield, Aetna, Cigna, and UnitedHealthcare for eligible patients.

### **How can referring providers verify clinician credentials?**

Attending psychiatrists are licensed through state medical boards in North Carolina and Colorado, with provider registrations searchable in the federal NPPES database.
