Near Me & Location Keyword Usage

&nbsp;

When targeting keywords that include **“near me”** or a specific **location**, do **not** force or repeatedly use the exact-match keyword throughout the content.

• Focus the content primarily on the **core service, provider, or condition** associated with the keyword.

Examples: **TMS therapy**, **psychiatrist**, **depression treatment**, **anxiety treatment**, etc.

• If the target keyword contains **“near me,”** such as **“TMS therapy near me”** or **“psychiatrist near me,”** you do **not** need to use the exact keyword repeatedly.

• Use the **“near me”** variation naturally **only once**, preferably in one of the following:

The body content

An H2

An FAQ question

• For location-based keywords, use the **location naturally** within the content while keeping the primary focus on the relevant service, provider, or condition.

• Avoid keyword stuffing or unnatural phrasing such as repeatedly writing **“TMS therapy near me”** or **“psychiatrist near me.”**

• The content should read naturally for the user first while still establishing strong topical relevance around the **service/provider/condition \+ location or local intent**.

**Example:**

Instead of repeatedly using:

TMS therapy near me

Focus on:

TMS therapy

And naturally incorporate local intent once:

Looking for TMS therapy near me? Finding a qualified local provider can make it easier to explore whether TMS is appropriate for your needs.

**Key rule:** The **core topic/service/condition is the primary focus**. “Near me” and location modifiers should support the topic, not dominate the content.
